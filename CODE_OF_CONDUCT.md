# Code of Conduct

Kitcount is built in the spirit of harm reduction: meeting people where they
are, with dignity and without judgement. We expect the same of everyone in
this project.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and kind, especially to people new to open source or to software.
- Use person-first, non-stigmatising language (for example "people who use
  drugs", not "addicts").
- Assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Protect privacy: never share anyone's personal information, and never post
  real participant data, tokens or enrollment codes.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, stigmatising
remarks about drug use or the people we serve, personal attacks, and publishing
others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Kitcount/security/advisories/new)
(mark it as a conduct report) or by contacting the maintainer directly.
Maintainers will review reports promptly and confidentially. They may remove
contributions or ban participants who break this code.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
